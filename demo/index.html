LISBON / FIELD NOTE

The hour matters.
So does the context.

Three public listings. Two cultural institutions. One practical lesson: a local clock time needs a time zone before it becomes a reliable event timestamp.

Our first import preserves the displayed local times. The reviewed version explicitly interprets them in Lisbon’s time zone. Try inputs D and C to see the difference.

Research snapshot: 15 September 2026. Event availability was not verified. Film running times were not treated as complete session lengths.
