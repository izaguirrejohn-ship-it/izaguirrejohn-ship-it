A SMALL TEST / A USEFUL NEXT ITERATION

Try the work.
Tell me where it breaks.

Give one demo ten minutes. Say what you expect to happen, try the tasks below, and note anything that makes you stop.

Use the supplied fictional examples. You don’t need an account, installation or your own company data. This guide does not record or submit your answers.

First, a quick impression.

Open the portfolio. Before opening a demo, answer: what does John do, and which project would you explore first? What made that clear or unclear?

OPTION A / SIGNAL ATLAS

Review an event feed.

Open Signal Atlas ↗
  1. Choose A · A feed with problems and check it. Find one issue and explain what an editor should inspect next.
  2. Switch to B · A consistent feed and check again. Explain what changed and what a clean result tells you.
  3. Download a report. Could you hand it to someone else and explain where it came from?

OPTION B / NIVQRA

Inspect an agent’s boundaries.

Open NIVQRA ↗
  1. Choose B · A missing approval owner and prepare a review. Which decision is unresolved, and who would need to resolve it?
  2. Inspect the permissions and scenarios. What, if anything, has the tool authorized or tested?
  3. Download the readable review. What would you do with it next?

Five questions worth answering.

  1. What did you think this tool would do?
  2. Where did you hesitate or need an explanation?
  3. What did the result prove? What did it leave unanswered?
  4. When would you use this in your own work, if ever?
  5. What is the single change that would make it more useful?

Include your device and browser if something broke. Keep any example data fictional and leave out confidential information.

For the person running the session.

Let the tester work before offering help. Record where they stopped and whether they completed each task unaided. Keep their notes private.

Open after the tasks: interpretation checks
  • Signal Atlas: A has 7 errors and 1 warning; B has no findings under its preset review settings. Passing checks does not verify that an event will happen or that tickets are available.
  • NIVQRA: B is missing a new-counterparty approver. Its amount approver remains separate. Every output is inactive, and scenario specifications have not been executed.
  • If a tester reads a result as verified event truth or active authority, record a comprehension problem even when every button works.
