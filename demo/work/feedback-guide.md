Prepared 15 September 2026. Status: ready to use; no participant sessions completed or invitations sent by this build.

Portfolio: https://izaguirrejohn-ship-it.github.io/izaguirrejohn-ship-it/work/

## Aim

Learn whether visitors understand John's work, can complete a useful demo task without help, and interpret its output correctly. Five sessions are an initial usability exercise, not statistical validation or customer traction.

## Suggested participant mix

Recruit five people who can give concrete feedback. Aim for two culture/editorial operators for Signal Atlas, two people designing or operating agent workflows for NIVQRA, and one founder or product leader for the overall portfolio. These are recruitment criteria, not identified or confirmed participants.

Each session takes approximately 15 minutes. Use the fictional sample data. Capture anonymous participant labels (P1–P5), device/browser, observed behavior and a short quote only with the participant's agreement. Store completed notes privately; do not commit personal feedback to this public repository.

## Session sequence

1. Show the portfolio for a first impression. Ask what John does and which project they would explore. Don't explain it first.
2. Pick one demo matched to their work. Ask them to narrate what they expect and what they see.
3. Give the tasks below one at a time. Observe before helping; record any assistance.
4. Ask the five closing questions.
5. Summarize one concrete friction point, its impact and a possible correction. Separate what you observed from what you inferred.

### Signal Atlas tasks

- Check A, the fictional feed with problems. Identify one issue and explain what to inspect next.
- Check B, the consistent fictional feed. Explain what changed and what a clean result establishes.
- Download a report and explain how you would hand it to a colleague.

Facilitator interpretation: A returns 7 errors and 1 warning; B returns no findings under its preset settings. A clean report does not verify event truth or ticket availability.

### NIVQRA tasks

- Prepare B, the missing-owner example. Identify the unresolved approval responsibility.
- Inspect the permissions and scenarios. Explain what has been authorized or tested.
- Download the readable review and describe the next human step.

Facilitator interpretation: the new-counterparty approver is missing; the amount approver is separate. All outputs remain DRAFT · NOT ACTIVE. Scenario specifications are not executed tests, and no authority is activated.

## Closing questions

1. What did you expect this tool to do?
2. Where did you hesitate or need an explanation?
3. What did the result prove, and what did it leave unanswered?
4. When would you use it in your own work, if ever?
5. What single change would make it more useful?

## Review after the five sessions

Prioritize blockers, misleading result interpretation, and repeated friction. Investigate a severe misunderstanding even if observed once. Keep aesthetic preferences separate from task failures. Choose the smallest correction that addresses the evidence, then retest that task with an appropriate participant. Record the result in the relevant project notes; do not claim validation from opinions alone.

## Collection route

Participants may choose to email their notes to hello@iov.agency. The public page has no submission endpoint or analytics. Email opens only on the participant's action and sends only when they choose to send. This plan does not schedule sessions, send invitations or establish demand.
